# Visit Kirtland: privacy notice review

Proposed route: /privacy/. Source: [draft notice](../../content/privacy.html). This site had no configured analytics privacy-notice route; a review-only draft has been added.

The consent interface links to this route, and public pages expose a Privacy notice link and Tracking preferences button even while the feature is disabled. Drafts are visibly marked not effective and noindex. No notice has been published.

Site-specific wording covers historical 2026 reunion navigation, no configured saved-form/completion hook, external visitor services and an unresolved privacy contact. The notices distinguish operational requests from optional analytics and external transactions.

All notices describe GA4, Meta, Metricool, cookies/identifiers, opt-in, withdrawal/reload, browser storage, 180-day consent persistence, privacy signals, the fact that existing cookies are not deleted, and the separate status of Cloudflare analytics. Metricool's no-cookie description is attributed to the vendor rather than inferred from its name.

Validation: 34 analytics/configuration tests; 4 desktop / 4 mobile; notice routes render at desktop/mobile widths with one main landmark, unique IDs, no horizontal notice overflow and no page exceptions. See [validation evidence](TRACKING_VALIDATION_REPORT.md).
