Visit Kirtland: privacy and tracking notice

This proposed notice describes optional measurement on Visit Kirtland. It is a draft for review and has not taken effect.

Visitor information and reunion links

The proposed custom events identify clicks to the historical 2026 family-reunion registration pages or app. These clicks show navigation interest. The separate reunion app has no verified completion hook in this implementation, so we do not report them as completed registrations. No saved-form event is configured for this static visitor-information site.

Links to lodging, restaurants, attractions, reunion services and other third-party websites open services with their own policies. The proposed notice does not establish how those independent services handle bookings or form information. Existing hosting and independent Cloudflare Web Analytics remain separate from these optional additions.

Privacy contact and organizational responsibility for this notice must be confirmed before it takes effect. Until then, use the website’s existing contact information for questions.

Your optional tracking choices

Optional analytics and marketing tracking is disabled in this review version. If approved and enabled later, it waits for your choice. Choose “Decline optional”, “Allow analytics”, or “Allow analytics and marketing”. Accepting a choice cannot switch on a disabled service. You can use this website and follow its booking or registration links without accepting optional tracking.

Use the “Tracking preferences” button at the bottom of a page to reopen your choices. Preferences are stored on this website in your browser for up to 180 days. They apply across pages on the same website origin; another website, browser, device, or www/non-www origin may ask again. An expired, missing or unreadable preference asks for a new choice before optional tracking starts. A supported Global Privacy Control signal or Do Not Track preference keeps these optional services off.

What the proposed services measure

Google Analytics 4 would measure page views and the selected website interactions described below. Our custom events contain page paths, approved campaign labels, event information, link destinations, and the type of successful form submission. Form answers, names, email addresses and internal submission keys are not included in these custom event payloads. Google’s automatic measurement can collect additional interactions; its settings still require review before activation.

Google Analytics can use first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_<identifier> to distinguish visits and sessions. Google documents default cookie lifetimes of up to two years, subject to settings and browser restrictions. Our 180-day consent preference is separate from those cookies.

Meta Pixel would measure page views and approved campaign-interest events only after marketing consent. Meta may use advertising identifiers and cookies such as _fbp and, where applicable, _fbc. Its account, cookie settings and exact enabled behavior require approval before use. Automatic Meta event configuration is disabled by our loader. These vendor requests can expose network and browser information to the provider; we do not describe them as anonymous.

Metricool would measure website visits after analytics consent. Metricool describes its website tracker as using no cookies and deriving country and viewed-page information through the IP address. Its tag is still unverified for this website. We do not send our custom conversion events to Metricool. A website visit does not establish a completed sale.

For Google’s page-view configuration and custom events we keep approved campaign labels and strip other query parameters and fragments. The Meta and Metricool loaders are suppressed when the page or referrer contains unapproved query parameters or fragments, because those services can read the actual URL. This protection is not a guarantee about every automatic vendor feature.

Changing or withdrawing a choice

Open “Tracking preferences” and choose a more restrictive option to withdraw consent. The page reloads after a withdrawal to stop the previously loaded optional scripts; future custom collection is blocked. This does not retract information already sent. This version does not delete existing vendor cookies. You can remove those cookies using your browser’s site-data controls; clearing all site data also removes your saved preference. If storage is unavailable, a preference may not persist.

The proposed consent control covers the three optional services above. Hosting, security, operational form processing, and any existing independent Cloudflare Web Analytics are separate. It does not control an external provider after you follow a link.
